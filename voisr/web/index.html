          Dictation

          Clipboard fallback

          Copied when auto-paste is off or blocked. After a successful auto-paste your previous clipboard is put back, so the dictation is not left on it.

          Automatic

          Auto-paste in hotkey mode

          After a push-to-talk dictation, paste the text straight into the app you're in (Cmd-V). Needs Accessibility permission.

          How consecutive dictations join

          Dictations within five minutes of each other are joined with a space unless you typed in between; a mouse click alone does not count, so you may see a leading space after clicking into a new field.

          Transcription model

          All models run on this Mac and are English only. Extra models are optional downloads.

          Keep model loaded

          Voisr loads the model when you start talking and keeps it in memory, so the next dictation skips the model load. That saved roughly half a second to a second in tests, and varies. While it is loaded it uses about 0.7 GB of memory for Small and 0.3 GB for Base (more for bigger models), then frees it after the time you pick. “Immediately” reloads the model for every dictation and keeps no memory. Everything stays on this Mac.

          Privacy & data

          Privacy

          Audio is recorded to a temp file, transcribed by whisper.cpp on this Mac, then deleted. No accounts, no telemetry, and your audio and text never leave this Mac.

          Local only

          Read the text around your cursor

          While you dictate, Voisr reads the app you are in: the field you are typing in, the window title and nearby labels. That spells names on screen correctly and checks that your paste landed. The text stays in memory for that dictation and is never saved. It never reads password fields or password managers. Off: Voisr reads no text from other apps.

          Learn from my corrections

          For up to a minute after a paste, Voisr re-reads that field to spot words you fixed, and suggests them in Dictionary. Only the corrected words are saved. Needs reading the text around your cursor.

          Add safe corrections automatically

          After you make the same fix twice, Voisr adds it to Dictionary without asking, if it only changes capitals or the spelling of one unusual word. Never common words or numbers. Everything else waits for you to accept it, and Dictionary marks what was added so you can remove it.

          Clear all history

          Permanently delete every stored dictation. This cannot be undone.
